## 🧠 Overview

**Boxcutter Blender Add-on** is a professional hard-surface modeling extension built to enhance Blender with fast and efficient shape creation tools. Designed for artists, designers, and 3D professionals, it enables rapid construction of mechanical forms, detailed assets, and production-ready models using an intuitive viewport-based workflow.

The add-on focuses on accelerating modeling operations through advanced boolean workflows, interactive cutters, customizable shape tools, and non-destructive editing techniques. Users can quickly block out concepts, create precise panel details, and refine complex geometry while maintaining full control over every stage of production.

Boxcutter integrates seamlessly into Blender's modeling environment, making it suitable for game asset creation, product visualization, concept design, motion graphics, architectural projects, and industrial modeling. Its streamlined workflow reduces repetitive tasks and allows creators to focus on design rather than technical limitations.

Whether you're developing detailed props, sci-fi environments, mechanical assemblies, or high-quality visualization assets, Boxcutter provides a powerful toolkit that improves productivity and simplifies hard-surface modeling workflows.
